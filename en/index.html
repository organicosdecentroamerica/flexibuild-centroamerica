Frequently asked questions

Working with FlexiBuild Centroamérica

Does FlexiBuild supply systems and also build projects?

Yes. System supply and project construction are separate services. Construction can be evaluated and quoted by request in Nicaragua, with scope and responsibilities agreed in writing.

Which countries do you serve?

ICF system supply can be evaluated throughout Central America. Modular Steel Framing and mobile business solutions are offered in Nicaragua, Costa Rica, Honduras and El Salvador, subject to project review.

Can realtors and real estate companies work with FlexiBuild?

Yes. We welcome inquiries from realtors, real estate agencies, property managers and developers who have clients or projects requiring construction systems, modular spaces or project evaluation. Any referral or collaboration arrangement must be agreed separately.

Is the Biomodular system available for construction?

No. It remains in development and requires technical validation and commercial readiness before it can be offered as an operational construction system.

What information is needed for a project review?

Please share the location, intended use, approximate dimensions, project stage, plans or site information if available, expected timeline and the service you are seeking.
