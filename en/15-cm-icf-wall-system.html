15 cm Core ICF Wall System

Insulating concrete form system for reinforced concrete walls. System selection and structural design must be completed by the project professionals.

Dimensional data and intended applications: Better Block® manufacturer product specifications.

Intended application and system configuration

The manufacturer identifies the 15 cm core option for residential exterior walls and multi-level applications. It provides the main wall configuration for coordinating structural perimeters with thinner interior partitions.

A 15 cm main wall can connect to a 10 cm interior partition using factory-made 15 × 10 cm T connectors. T configurations with a 15 cm branch are also available. Reinforcement, concrete, anchors, openings and foundations must be defined by the building's structural design.

Components and junctions

15 cm straight form; L-shaped exterior corner components; opening bucks; and 15 × 10 cm T connectors for interior partition junctions.

The Better Block® eighth-generation system includes factory-made T forms and pre-marked cutting guides at 20 cm intervals. These details support coordinated layout, accurate cutting and continuity at wall intersections.

Core thickness is only one design input. The structural engineer must define reinforcement, concrete mix, wall geometry, openings, connections, foundations and anchorage for the applicable loads and local requirements.
