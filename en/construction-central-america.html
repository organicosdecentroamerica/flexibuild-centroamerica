Project review

Country and location, intended use, dimensions, drawings, building levels, development stage and indicative budget.

2. System and scope

Preliminary review of suitable systems, logistics, technical requirements and services that must be contracted separately.

3. Proposal and responsibilities

Supply, included services, technical professionals, exclusions and project-specific execution conditions are documented.
