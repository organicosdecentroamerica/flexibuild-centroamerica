Mobile Businesses and Units

FlexiBuild develops mobile spaces for food service, retail, sales and mobile services. The layout, equipment, structure, trailer or vehicle, utilities and finishes are planned around the way each business operates.

Images on this page are visual references and conceptual renders; they do not necessarily represent completed or delivered projects. Country availability, transport, approvals and project scope are confirmed individually.

Mobile solutions

Design for the business operation

Start with the service or menu, equipment list, expected workflow, storage needs, location and how often the unit will move.

Food trucks and food trailers

Layouts coordinated around menu, cooking equipment, ventilation, water, power, cleaning and staff movement.

Food-service trailers

Food preparation, storage, service windows and equipment are defined for each operation.

Retail, kiosks and pop-up shops

Mobile points of sale, product displays and customer-service layouts planned around the merchandise.

Mobile services

Service units for personal care and other mobile operations, subject to equipment and local requirements.

Commercial trailers

Trailer and chassis configuration must be evaluated for loads, dimensions, stability and intended transport.

Project evaluation

Structure, weight and utilities

Each unit requires coordination of the base or chassis, load distribution, access, service windows, stability, electrical and water systems, ventilation, equipment and cleanable finishes. Road use requires confirmation of applicable dimensions, weight, hitch, lights, brakes and circulation requirements.
