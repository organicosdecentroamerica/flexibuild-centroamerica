20 cm Core ICF Wall System

Insulating concrete form system for reinforced concrete walls. System selection and structural design must be completed by the project professionals.

ICF system technical data

Concrete core20 cm
Overall form width33 cm
EPS thickness per side6.5 cm
Block length × height100 × 40 cm
EPS density26 kg/m³
Nominal form insulationR-22.5 (RSI ≈ 4.0)
Effective wall assembly insulationApproximately R-32 or higher, depending on finishes and detailing
Pre-marked cut guidesEvery 20 cm, aligned with internal webs

Dimensional data and intended applications are based on the published Better Block® product specifications. R-22.5 refers to the nominal insulation of the forms; the manufacturer estimates R-32 or higher for the completed wall assembly, depending on finishes and detailing. This indicative value does not replace a project-specific thermal calculation.

Intended application and system configuration

The manufacturer identifies the 20 cm core option for higher-load applications, multi-storey buildings and projects with demanding structural requirements. Its use requires the complete wall assembly and connections to be defined within the structural model.

T connectors can join 20 cm structural perimeters to 10 cm or 15 cm interior partitions, depending on the factory configuration. Wall design must account for gravity and lateral loads, reinforcement, concrete specification, junction continuity, foundations and anchorage.

Components and junctions

20 cm straight form; structural corner components; opening bucks; and 20 × 10 cm or 20 × 15 cm T connectors, depending on the junction.

The Better Block® eighth-generation system includes factory-made T forms and pre-marked cutting guides at 20 cm intervals. These details support coordinated layout, accurate cutting and continuity at wall intersections.

Core thickness is only one design input. The structural engineer must define reinforcement, concrete mix, wall geometry, openings, connections, foundations and anchorage for the applicable loads and local requirements.
