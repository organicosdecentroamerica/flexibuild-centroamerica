ICF Construction System · Better Block ICF®

Permanent intelligent formwork: reinforced concrete and insulation in one system

The EPS forms act as the mold during the pour and remain integrated on both sides of the wall. Reinforcing steel is placed inside, and concrete is poured according to the structural design. Better Block ICF® is an eighth-generation permanent formwork system: the insulated panels remain in place and help protect the monolithic concrete core and reinforcing steel from direct sunlight and environmental exposure. This can contribute to long-term preservation; waterproofing and protective details are designed for each project.

01 / PERMANENT FORM

The form stays in place

The EPS faces are not removed after the pour. They remain part of the insulated wall assembly, help reduce heat transfer and reduce direct environmental exposure of the concrete core and reinforcing steel.

02 / STRUCTURAL CORE

Reinforced-concrete wall

The core is formed by placing reinforcing steel and concrete inside the forms. Core thickness, reinforcement, connections and pour sequence are specified for each project.

03 / ASSEMBLY GEOMETRY

Integrated webs and guides

Internal plastic webs and cut guides help maintain form spacing and geometry. Reinforcement and construction details follow the project drawings.

Better Block’s published product sheet lists EPS at a nominal density of 26 kg/m³ and concrete-core options of 10, 15 or 20 cm. These are manufacturer data; structural capacity, insulation and finished-wall performance depend on the full design, materials, installation and local requirements. How it works

From project plans to an ICF wall

ICF blocks are assembled to form wall cavities. Reinforcing steel and coordinated services are installed before concrete placement. The insulated formwork remains in place. Wall thickness, reinforcement, concrete, bracing, pour sequence and connections must follow project design, manufacturer instructions and applicable codes.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about the ICF Construction System

What does ICF mean?

ICF means insulated concrete forms. The forms create a cavity for reinforcing steel and concrete and remain integrated with the wall.

What is Better Block ICF®?

Better Block ICF® is an eighth-generation permanent formwork system. Its insulated EPS panels remain around the monolithic reinforced-concrete core and provide thermal insulation.

How does the system help protect the concrete core and reinforcing steel?

Because the formwork stays in place, it helps reduce direct exposure of the concrete core and reinforcing steel to sunlight and weather. This can support wall durability; the project design must include appropriate waterproofing and protective details.

Is the Better Block ICF system earthquake resistant?

Yes. Better Block ICF can form part of an earthquake-resistant structural system: the reinforced-concrete wall must be engineered for project loads and site conditions. Performance depends on structural design, soil, foundation, reinforcement, connections, materials, construction, supervision and applicable codes.

What energy-efficiency and comfort benefits can it provide?

Continuous insulation can help stabilize indoor temperatures and reduce heating and cooling use. Under certain conditions, savings may reach up to 60%; actual results vary with climate, design, occupancy and equipment. The combination of concrete and EPS can also reduce some sound transmission.

Does the system by itself prevent mold or indoor-air-quality problems?

No. A well-designed wall can support thermal comfort and moisture control, but preventing condensation or mold also requires suitable ventilation, waterproofing, sound construction details and maintenance.

How much does it cost to build with Better Block ICF®?

Cost depends on design, wall area and height, reinforcement, concrete volume, logistics, labor and site conditions. For an initial estimate, send plans or approximate dimensions, location and intended use to FlexiBuild.
