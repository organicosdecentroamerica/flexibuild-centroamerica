ICF Construction · Nicaragua

FlexiBuild ICF® for reinforced-concrete walls

ICF means insulated concrete forms. FlexiBuild offers its eighth-generation system throughout Central America. FlexiBuild ICF® is permanent formwork: its insulated panels remain around a monolithic reinforced-concrete core. The system provides thermal and acoustic comfort and helps protect the core and reinforcing steel from direct exposure to sunlight, moisture and other environmental factors, supporting long-term preservation of the building’s structural characteristics. It is intended for high-performance walls under seismic and hurricane-wind demands established by each project’s structural design. FlexiBuild can quote construction when requested; reinforcement, location, logistics and execution are reviewed for each project.

ICF Construction System · FlexiBuild ICF®

Permanent intelligent formwork: reinforced concrete and insulation in one system

The EPS forms act as the mold during the pour and remain integrated on both sides of the wall. Reinforcing steel is placed inside, and concrete is poured according to the structural design. FlexiBuild ICF® is an eighth-generation permanent formwork system: the insulated panels stay in place to provide thermal and acoustic comfort and help protect the monolithic concrete core and reinforcing steel from direct exposure to sunlight, moisture and other environmental factors. This protection supports long-term preservation of the structure and its characteristics. Finishes and waterproofing are specified for each project.

01 / PERMANENT FORM

The form stays in place

The EPS faces are not removed after the pour. They remain part of the insulated wall assembly, help reduce heat transfer and reduce direct environmental exposure of the concrete core and reinforcing steel.

02 / STRUCTURAL CORE

Reinforced-concrete wall

The core is formed by placing reinforcing steel and concrete inside the forms. Core thickness, reinforcement, connections and pour sequence are specified for each project.

03 / ASSEMBLY GEOMETRY

Integrated webs and guides

Internal plastic webs and cut guides help maintain form spacing and geometry. Reinforcement and construction details follow the project drawings.

FlexiBuild’s published product sheet lists EPS at a nominal density of 26 kg/m³ and concrete-core options of 10, 15 or 20 cm. These are manufacturer data; structural capacity, insulation and finished-wall performance depend on the full design, materials, installation and local requirements. Concrete block masonry is familiar throughout the region. FlexiBuild ICF® combines permanent EPS formwork, continuous insulation and a monolithic reinforced-concrete core in one wall system. Wall thickness, reinforcement, concrete, bracing, pour sequence and connections must follow project design, manufacturer instructions and applicable codes.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about the ICF Construction System

What does ICF mean?

ICF means insulated concrete forms. The forms create a cavity for reinforcing steel and concrete and remain integrated with the wall.

What is FlexiBuild ICF®?

FlexiBuild ICF® is an eighth-generation permanent formwork system. Its insulated EPS panels remain around the monolithic reinforced-concrete core and provide thermal insulation.

How does the system help protect the concrete core and reinforcing steel?

Because the formwork stays in place, it helps reduce direct exposure of the concrete core and reinforcing steel to sunlight and weather. This can support wall durability; the project design must include appropriate waterproofing and protective details.

Is the FlexiBuild ICF system earthquake and hurricane resistant?

Yes. FlexiBuild ICF forms continuous reinforced-concrete walls that can be designed for the seismic and hurricane-wind demands at each location. Permanent formwork helps protect the monolithic core and reinforcing steel from direct exposure to sunlight, moisture and other environmental factors, supporting long-term preservation of the structure. Structural calculations specify core thickness, reinforcement, connections, foundation and roof and opening anchorage for local loads and codes.

What energy-efficiency and comfort benefits can it provide?

Continuous insulation can help stabilize indoor temperatures and reduce heating and cooling use. Under certain conditions, savings may reach up to 60%; actual results vary with climate, design, occupancy and equipment. The combination of concrete and EPS can also reduce some sound transmission.

Does the system by itself prevent mold or indoor-air-quality problems?

Insulation and wall continuity help maintain more stable temperatures and reduce conditions that contribute to condensation. Ventilation, waterproofing, air quality and maintenance are coordinated with each building’s design to support a healthy and comfortable indoor environment.

How much does it cost to build with FlexiBuild ICF®?

Cost depends on design, wall area and height, reinforcement, concrete volume, logistics, labor and site conditions. For an initial estimate, send plans or approximate dimensions, location and intended use to FlexiBuild.

How does ICF differ from conventional concrete block?

FlexiBuild ICF® combines permanent EPS formwork, continuous insulation and a monolithic reinforced-concrete core in one wall system. Conventional block is built unit by unit, with reinforcement, grout and insulation specified by the design. Both require structural engineering; ICF’s distinction is the integration of structure and insulation.
