Getting started

Common questions

What information should I send?

Country and location, intended use, approximate area or dimensions, number of levels, drawings or sketches, project stage and preferred timing. A reference budget is helpful if available.

Can FlexiBuild build in all four countries?

Construction is available by request with the relevant FlexiBuild systems, subject to agreed scope, local requirements and project review. ICF is offered in Nicaragua and Honduras only; Better Block ICF® commercial representation is currently in Nicaragua. Biomodular remains in development.

Can realtors and real estate companies work with FlexiBuild?

Yes, we welcome project inquiries and possible collaboration with real estate professionals. Any referral or service arrangement is discussed and agreed separately.

Can FlexiBuild provide design or construction services?

FlexiBuild can quote project construction when requested. Design, permits, engineering review, qualified professionals and construction responsibilities are defined for each project under local requirements.
